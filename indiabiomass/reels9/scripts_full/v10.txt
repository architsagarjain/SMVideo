Five a.m. An aggregator's day starts with phone calls. Which farms have finished harvesting. Which balers are free.
Eight a.m. Out on the fields, checking the straw. Is it dry? Is it clean? Wet bales lose value.
Eleven a.m. Agreeing terms with farmers, one field at a time.
Two p.m. Arranging trucks. Too few, and the bales sit in the rain. Too many, and the trucks wait empty.
Five p.m. Calling buyers. Who needs how much, and who pays on time.
Nine p.m. Planning tomorrow.
Aggregators hold the biomass supply chain together. Most of that work still happens on one phone, one call at a time.
IndiaBiomass is building one place for the people who keep India's biomass moving.
