Three questions people ask us about the biomass business.
One. Can I sell biomass without owning a factory?
Yes. Many people collect, bale and store crop residue, and supply it to pellet makers and plants. That is aggregation, and it is a business on its own.
Two. Why does the price change so much?
Season, region, quality and distance. Straw is cheap at harvest and costly months later. And over long distances, transport can cost as much as the fuel itself.
Three. How do I find buyers outside my district?
Today, mostly through contacts and calls. That is exactly the gap we are working on.
