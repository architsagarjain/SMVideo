Biomass has seasons, just like crops. Here is the year in under a minute.
October and November. The paddy harvest in the north. Paddy straw is everywhere, and time is short.
From November, sugar mills start crushing. Bagasse piles up until spring.
Through winter, cotton is picked, and cotton stalks are cleared from the fields.
February to April. Mustard is threshed, then wheat is harvested. Mustard husk and wheat straw come in.
And rice husk? It comes all year, wherever rice mills are running.
Buy in season, and supply is easy. Buy out of season, and you compete for what is left.
