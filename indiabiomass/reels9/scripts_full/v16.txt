Five things you probably did not know about biomass. Quickly.
One. Many sugar mills run on their own waste. They burn bagasse to make their steam and power.
Two. Rice husk ash goes into cement and steel making.
Three. Coconut shells become activated carbon, the kind used in water filters.
Four. Paddy straw can be turned into compressed biogas. C-B-G plants are already running on it.
Five. In the hills, fallen pine needles are being pressed into briquettes.
Fuel, filter, gas, building material. The same residue can go a dozen ways.
The question is who needs it, and where.
IndiaBiomass is building the way to find out.
