One word you will hear more often in biomass. Torrefaction.
It means roasting biomass at a high temperature, with very little oxygen. Not burning it. Roasting it.
What comes out is dark, dry and brittle. Almost like coal.
Why does that matter?
It packs more heat into every kilo.
It does not soak up water, so it stores better.
And it grinds easily, which power plants like, because their mills are built for coal.
