Four red flags to watch for in any biomass deal.
Red flag one. No specs in writing. If moisture, ash and heat value are not written down, every dispute becomes your word against theirs.
Red flag two. A price far below everyone else. Very cheap fuel often means wet, mixed, or not available at all.
Red flag three. Full payment in advance, to someone you have never met or verified.
Red flag four. No delivery schedule. Without dates and quantities, a deal is just a conversation.
One red flag is a reason to ask more questions. Two is a reason to walk away.
