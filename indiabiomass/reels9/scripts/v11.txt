Four red flags in any biomass deal.
One. No specs in writing. If moisture, ash and heat value are not written down, every dispute is your word against theirs.
Two. A price far below everyone else. Very cheap fuel often means wet, mixed, or not there at all.
Three. Full payment in advance, to someone you have never verified.
Four. No delivery schedule. Without dates and quantities, a deal is just a conversation.
One red flag means ask more questions. Two means walk away.
