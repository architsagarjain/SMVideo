Loose straw goes in. Hard, shiny pellets come out. Here is what happens in between.
Step one. Cleaning. Stones, soil and metal are removed, because they damage the machines.
Step two. Drying. Pellets need low moisture to hold together.
Step three. Grinding. A hammer mill turns the straw into a fine powder.
Step four. Pressing. Rollers force the powder through small holes in a steel die. Heat and pressure make it bind.
Step five. Cooling. Fresh pellets come out hot and soft. Cooling makes them hard.
Step six. Screening and bagging. Dust is removed, and the pellets are packed for the buyer.
Six steps, and a waste becomes a fuel.
