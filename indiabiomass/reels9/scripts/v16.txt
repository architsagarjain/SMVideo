Five things you probably did not know about biomass.
One. Many sugar mills run on their own waste, burning bagasse for steam and power.
Two. Rice husk ash goes into cement and steel making.
Three. Coconut shells become activated carbon, used in water filters.
Four. Paddy straw can be turned into compressed biogas. C-B-G plants already run on it.
Five. In the hills, fallen pine needles are pressed into briquettes.
The same residue can go a dozen ways.
The question is who needs it, and where.
IndiaBiomass is building the way to find out.
