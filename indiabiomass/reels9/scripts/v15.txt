Three questions people ask us about biomass.
One. Can I sell biomass without a factory?
Yes. Many people collect, bale and store crop residue, and supply pellet makers and plants. That is aggregation, a business on its own.
Two. Why does the price change so much?
Season, region, quality and distance. Straw is cheap at harvest and costly months later.
Three. How do I find buyers outside my district?
Today, mostly through contacts and calls. That is exactly the gap we are working on.
