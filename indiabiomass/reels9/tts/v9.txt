Loose straw goes in. Hard pellets come out. Here is what happens in between.
Step one. Cleaning. Stones and metal are removed.
Step two. Drying. Pellets need low moisture to hold together.
Step three. Grinding. A hammer mill turns the straw into powder.
Step four. Pressing. Rollers force it through a steel die. Heat and pressure make it bind.
Step five. Cooling. Hot, soft pellets turn hard.
Step six. Screening and bagging, ready for the buyer.
Six steps, and a waste becomes a fuel.
