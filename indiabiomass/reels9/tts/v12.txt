Bio-mass has seasons, just like crops.
October and November. The paddy harvest in the north. Paddy straw everywhere, and little time.
From November, sugar mills start crushing. Bagasse piles up until spring.
Through winter, cotton stalks are cleared from the fields.
February to April. Mustard husk, then wheat straw.
And rice husk comes all year, wherever rice mills run.
Buy in season, and supply is easy. Buy out of season, and you compete for what is left.
Plan your year around the calendar.
