Seven a.m. Two people, two districts apart.
In Punjab, a pellet maker has a warehouse full of stock, and no one to sell to.
In Haryana, a factory manager needs fuel by Friday, and no one to buy from.
She calls three traders. He checks old WhatsApp groups.
She hears three different prices. He gets no reply.
By evening, his boiler runs short. Her stock is still waiting.
They just never found each other.
That is the real gap in India's bio-mass trade. Not supply. Not demand. The connection.
IndiaBiomass is building it.
