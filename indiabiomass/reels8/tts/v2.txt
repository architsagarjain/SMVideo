Four things people get wrong about the bio-mass business.
Myth one. Bio-mass is just waste.
Fact. Once it is collected, dried and processed, it is a fuel, with a spec sheet, a price, and buyers waiting for it.
Myth two. Only big companies can sell to power plants.
Fact. Many tenders are open to smaller suppliers, and registered M-S-Es can often skip the deposit.
Myth three. All straw is the same.
Fact. Moisture, ash and heat value decide what your fuel is worth. Buyers test every load.
Myth four. The price is whatever the buyer says.
Fact. Prices change by region and by season. The real problem is that most sellers cannot see them.
