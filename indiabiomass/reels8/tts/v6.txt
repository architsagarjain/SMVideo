The rice is cut. The clock starts.
In north India, farmers have only a few weeks between the paddy harvest and sowing wheat.
The field has to be cleared. And the straw left behind is bulky, loose, and slow to move.
Without a baler, a buyer, or a truck, the fastest option is a matchstick.
That is why the smoke comes back every autumn.
Now, the same straw, with a plan.
The harvest ends. A baler arrives and packs the straw. Within days, an aggregator picks it up. The field is clear in time for wheat.
The straw becomes pellets. The pellets go to a plant that needs them.
Same field. Same few weeks. A different ending.
What decides it is whether the farmer can find a baler and a buyer in time.
IndiaBiomass is building that connection.
