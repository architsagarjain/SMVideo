This is a bale of paddy straw. A month ago, it was standing in a field in Punjab. Here is where it goes next.
After the harvest, a baler presses the loose straw into tight bales, so it can be moved instead of burned.
An aggregator collects bales from dozens of farms and stacks them at a depot, dry and covered.
From there, a truck takes them to a pellet plant. The straw is shredded, dried, and pressed into small, dense pellets.
The pellets are tested. Heat value. Moisture. Ash. Then they are loaded for a power plant that must burn bio-mass along with coal.
One bale. Five businesses. Farmer, baler, aggregator, pellet maker, buyer.
Every link has to find the next one.
That is the chain IndiaBiomass is being built to connect.
