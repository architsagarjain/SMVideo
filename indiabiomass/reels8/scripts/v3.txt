Pellet or briquette. Both are pressed biomass, but they are not the same product.
Pellets are small, about the size of a pencil stub. They flow easily and feed automatically, which is why power plants co-firing biomass mostly ask for pellets.
Briquettes are bigger, like short logs. They are popular with industrial boilers, brick kilns and factories.
Pellets need finer, drier raw material and more processing, so a pellet plant costs more to set up.
Briquette machines are simpler, which makes them an easier place to start.
So which one should you make?
It depends on two things. The raw material you have, and who is buying near you.
