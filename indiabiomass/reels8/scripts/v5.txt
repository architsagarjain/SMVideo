Before a buyer pays for your biomass, they check five things. Know them before you quote.
One. Moisture. Wet fuel burns poorly, and you end up transporting water. Most buyers set a maximum.
Two. G-C-V. The heat your fuel gives per kilo. Below the minimum, the load can be rejected, or the price cut.
Three. Ash. More ash means more cleaning, and more wear on the boiler.
Four. Size. Pellets and briquettes must match the size the buyer's feeding system takes.
Five. Consistency. One good sample is not enough. Every truck has to match the spec.
Get these five right, and price talks get a lot easier.
